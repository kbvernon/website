Research Interests

Broadly speaking, my research focuses on the ecology of human behavior, but here are the specific questions that really animate me these days:

  1. Climate adaptation and migration: in very abstract terms, how do people distribute themselves across space, and how is that influenced over the long term by climate trends?
  2. The economics of early, early, early urbanism: that is, how did we get from small, dispersed groups of mobile hunter-gatherers to large concentrations of sedentary city dwellers?
  3. Demographic interpolation: or, how do we estimate population size without a census?

In my spare time, I also contribute to several FOSS projects in the R community, especially extendr.
